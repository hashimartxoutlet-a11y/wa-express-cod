Shopify Connection & Store Settings

Connect Shopify Guide (Dev Dashboard Mode A)
  1. Open dev.shopify.com/dashboard with the account that owns the store.
  2. Create an app: set App URL to https://shopify.dev/apps/default-app-home, UNCHECK "Embed app in Shopify admin", UNCHECK "Use legacy install flow", and add no webhooks.
  3. Go to Versions: add scopes read_products and write_orders, then click Release.
  4. Install the app on your store from the app Home page.
  5. Go to Settings: copy the Client ID and Client Secret and paste them below.

Note: Shopify UI wording may change slightly.

Quick Help & FAQ

1. How do orders appear in Shopify? As normal pending COD orders with tags wa-express-cod, COD, and inventory decremented per your product policy.

2. What happens if I hit my monthly limit? On Growth/Agency, overage kicks in up to 20%/25%. After the hard cap, customers automatically fall back to WhatsApp-only mode so you never lose a sale.

3. WhatsApp Quick Replies: Use the Links tab to copy a pre-formatted Roman-Urdu/English reply and save it in WhatsApp Business Quick Replies (e.g. /order).
